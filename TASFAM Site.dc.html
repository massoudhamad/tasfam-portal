Reported by the Paje seaweed group, verified at the cooperative. Imeripotiwa na kikundi cha mwani cha Paje, imethibitishwa na chama cha ushirika.

For eleven years Mwanaidi Juma farmed seaweed in the shallows off Paje, walking out at low tide to tie cuttings between wooden pegs. From about 2019 the crop began to fail in the hot months: the water in the lagoon grew too warm, the seaweed bleached and broke apart before harvest. Kwa miaka kumi na moja Mwanaidi Juma alilima mwani katika maji mafupi ya Paje, akitembea maji yanapopwa kufunga miche kati ya vigingi. Kuanzia mwaka 2019 mazao yalianza kuharibika miezi ya joto: maji ya rasi yalipata joto kupita kiasi, mwani ukabadilika rangi na kuvunjika kabla ya mavuno.

In 2025 her group was one of forty-six selected under the project’s aquaculture component. They received tubular net lines designed for deeper water, shared access to a boat, swimming and safety training, and two seasons of extension support from the Department of Fisheries Development and Aquaculture. Mwaka 2025 kikundi chake kilikuwa mojawapo ya vikundi arobaini na sita vilivyochaguliwa chini ya kipengele cha ufugaji. Walipata mistari ya nyavu kwa maji ya kina, matumizi ya pamoja ya mashua, mafunzo ya kuogelea na usalama, na misimu miwili ya ushauri wa kitaalamu.

“The first time I swam out to the lines I was afraid. Now I go twice a week and I know what the crop will be before I pull it up.” “Mara ya kwanza kuogelea hadi mistarini niliogopa. Sasa naenda mara mbili kwa wiki na najua mazao yatakuwa vipi kabla ya kuyavuta.”

Two harvests later her seasonal yield had risen from 0.6 to 1.9 tonnes of dried seaweed. The group now sells through a single aggregated contract rather than to individual buyers at the roadside, and holds a share of the receipts in a joint account for line replacement. Baada ya mavuno mawili mavuno yake ya msimu yalipanda kutoka tani 0.6 hadi 1.9 za mwani mkavu. Kikundi kinauza kwa mkataba mmoja wa pamoja badala ya wanunuzi wa barabarani, na huweka sehemu ya mapato katika akaunti ya pamoja kwa ajili ya kubadilisha mistari.

About the projectKuhusu mradi

The Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries (Mainland) and the Ministry of Blue Economy and Fisheries (Zanzibar), through the Ministry of Finance, requested World Bank support for the Tanzania Scaling-up Sustainable Marine Fisheries and Aquaculture Management Project. Wizara ya Mifugo na Uvuvi (Bara) na Wizara ya Uchumi wa Buluu na Uvuvi (Zanzibar), kupitia Wizara ya Fedha, ziliomba msaada wa Benki ya Dunia kwa Mradi wa Kuimarisha Usimamizi Endelevu wa Uvuvi wa Baharini na Ufugaji wa Viumbe vya Maji Tanzania.

The project development objective is to improve the management effectiveness of selected priority fisheries at regional, national and community level. It supports sustainable fisheries management, strengthens sector governance, and promotes shared growth by harnessing the value of coastal and marine fisheries to national economies. Lengo la mradi ni kuboresha ufanisi wa usimamizi wa uvuvi wa kipaumbele katika ngazi za kikanda, kitaifa na kijamii. Unasaidia usimamizi endelevu wa uvuvi, kuimarisha utawala wa sekta, na kuchochea ukuaji shirikishi kwa kutumia thamani ya uvuvi wa pwani na bahari.
